OUTPUT STRUCTURE

A good research record should make your reasoning visible.

The goal is not to fill boxes. The goal is to preserve the logic behind your analysis.

01

Research question

What are you trying to understand, compare, identify, or test?

02

Input & method

What data did you use and which tools or methods did you apply?

03

Observation

What did the analysis actually show?

04

Evidence

What supports the observation, and what type of evidence is it?

05

Interpretation

What biological meaning can reasonably be proposed?

06

Limitations

What remains uncertain, missing, or unsupported?

07

Next question

What should you investigate next based on what you found?

08

References

Keep the database records, papers, structures, tools, or sources behind your interpretation.
