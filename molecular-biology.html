HOW TO USE A TECHNIQUE

A technique is only useful when its output answers a research question.

01

Define the question

What biological variable are you trying to measure, detect, compare or localize?

02

Choose the method

Match the method to the biological question, sample type, target and required evidence.

03

Build controls

Controls help distinguish a true biological signal from technical failure, contamination or background.

04

Interpret the evidence

Separate what was directly measured from what is inferred from the result.
