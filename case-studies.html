EVIDENCE MODEL

Different evidence types answer different questions.

نوع شواهد مشخص می‌ماند: مشاهده، پیش‌بینی، annotation و ادعای مقاله یکی نیستند.

EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE

What was experimentally observed?

Experimental measurements or experimentally determined structures provide observations under defined conditions, with their own controls and limitations.

COMPUTATIONAL EVIDENCE

What does the analysis predict or suggest?

Includes sequence similarity, structural prediction, statistical analysis, and other model- or algorithm-based results.

DATABASE ANNOTATION

What has been recorded or curated?

Database records can provide identifiers, annotations, sequence information, structural metadata, and links to supporting resources.

LITERATURE-SUPPORTED

What has been reported in the literature?

Published studies provide context, reported findings, methods, interpretations, and limitations that should be evaluated in their original context.
