WHY WORKSPACE?

Research is more than the final answer.

A useful research record should preserve how you moved from a question to evidence and from evidence to an interpretation.

01

Research question

Record exactly what you were trying to find out before choosing a tool or analysis.

02

Evidence

Keep track of database records, experimental results, computational predictions, and literature-supported claims.

03

Interpretation

Separate what you directly observed from what you infer from those observations.

04

Limitations

Record what your current evidence cannot establish. This is part of the scientific result.

RESEARCH RECORD

A research record should tell the whole reasoning chain.

Here is an example of how a computational protein investigation could be documented.

EXAMPLE RECORD

Investigating a protein of unknown function

Example
Research question What can the sequence tell us about the possible function of this protein?
Input Protein sequence
Workflow BLAST → Domains → Structure
Evidence Database records + computational predictions
Observation Sequence similarity and a predicted structural fold
Interpretation A functional hypothesis supported by multiple evidence types
Limitation Computational evidence alone does not establish biological function.
Next question Which additional evidence could test the hypothesis?
